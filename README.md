## Writing your part of the SRS

Each member owns parts of `M1/SRS.md`, listed in [`M1/OWNERS.md`](M1/OWNERS.md). Edit only your own parts and your own rows in shared tables and in the Team table above.

To have an AI agent draft your part, run the `fill-srs` skill from the repo root: `/fill-srs` in Claude Code, or ask Codex, Copilot, Cursor or Gemini CLI to "use the fill-srs skill to fill in my part of the SRS". It interviews you, shows you the draft for approval, logs the AI use in `AI-USE.md` and pushes to `Itr1`. Setup and details are in [`docs/TEAM-GUIDE.md`](docs/TEAM-GUIDE.md); rules for agents are in [`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md).
